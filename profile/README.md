# Story Damage

**Good stories leave a mark.**

Story Damage is an independent storytelling studio and technology company creating original stories and experiences. We’re drawn to complicated people, distinctive worlds, and moments that refuse to stay neat.

At our core, we’re both storytellers and builders. We care about narrative and the systems that help stories get made. We ask why the process works the way it does, and we build thoughtful ways to support creativity without getting in its way.

The goal isn’t the machinery, it’s the work. We want stories with personality that feel lived in.

We take the craft seriously, ourselves a little less.

**Original stories, human stakes, a little damage.**
